# Brag Book Playbooks

Output formats for Retrieve mode. Use only entries from the brag book.

## 1:1 update

Three to five bullets from the last 1 to 2 weeks.

- What I did: one line per win, with the result
- What's next: one line
- Where I need help: one line, if any

## Promotion packet

Group entries under the criteria for the next level. If the company has a career level guide, use its headings.

For each criterion:
- The claim: one sentence on how Michael meets it
- Two or three entries as evidence, each with its number and proof
- Who can confirm it

End with gaps: criteria with weak or no evidence.

## Resume bullets

One bullet per entry. Use the personal copy only.

Format: verb + what + number + scope.
Example: "Automated weekly pipeline reporting, cutting prep time ~80% for a 4-person team."

Keep each bullet to one line. No client names or internal figures.

## Interview story (STAR)

- Situation: the context, in one or two sentences
- Task: what Michael was responsible for
- Action: what he did, step by step
- Result: the number, plus what changed for others

Pick entries with a clear before and after. Note likely follow-up questions.

## Performance review

Cover the full review period. Group by project or by company goal. Lead each group with the strongest entry. Include praises as quotes.

# Sanitization Rules

Use these to turn a work copy into a personal copy. When the company's contract or handbook says something stricter, follow the company.

## The test

Would Michael be comfortable saying this line in an interview with a competitor? If yes, it can go in the personal copy.

## Can go in the personal copy

- What Michael did and his role in it
- Results as percentages or rounded numbers ("cut prep time ~80%")
- Skills and tools
- Stakeholders by role ("Head of Sales")
- Praise, paraphrased and credited by role

## Stays in the work copy

- Client and partner names
- Exact revenue, budgets, or internal financials
- Unreleased products, plans, or strategy
- Internal project code names
- Code, documents, and screenshots
- Praise quoted word for word when it mentions any of the above

## How to rewrite

| Work copy | Personal copy |
|---|---|
| Closed NT$4.2M renewal with Acme Corp | Secured a major enterprise renewal |
| Project Falcon launch | New product launch |
| Jane Lin, VP Sales | VP Sales |
| Cut costs by NT$380K a year | Cut team tooling costs ~30% |

## Never

- Sync the work copy to personal storage automatically
- Forward work email or files to a personal account
- Upload the work copy to a personal AI tool
